Instructions

How to play

  1. Blocks fall into the well one at a time.
  2. Move and turn each block to fit it in place. Fill a whole row to clear it and score points.
  3. The taller your stack, the more every block and row is worth, up to ×4. Watch the marks on the right of the well.
  4. The game ends when the blocks reach the top.

On a touchscreen: use the buttons under the well (hold Left, Right or Down to keep moving), or tap the well to turn, swipe sideways to move and swipe down to drop.
Points: 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 for clearing 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 rows at once, times your level and height bonus. Dropping blocks fast earns a little extra.
